SWF testbeds

Shapes and fills
t_rect One filled rectangle. The simplest thing that can be wrong.
t_hole A fill with a hole, so the winding rule is exercised.
t_share Two fills sharing an edge - neither may bleed into the other.
t_circle Quadratic curves, flattened to chords.
t_bowtie A self-crossing contour. Without a band split this is nonsense, not slightly wrong.
t_layers Three style layers, drawn in order.
t_stroke Round caps and joins. A wrongly wound join punches a notch.
t_alpha Alpha compositing.
t_grad_l Linear gradient with its own matrix. Sampled from a 256-entry ramp texture.
t_grad_r Radial gradient. A picture of the gradient square, not a row.

Timeline, masks and characters
t_place Place, move, colour transform, remove - across four frames. This is the one that moves.
t_text Glyphs placed by advance. Two squares that must not merge into one.
t_clip A clip depth. The mask itself must cover zero pixels.
t_clipn Nested masks, which intersect.
t_morph A morph blended at the ratio the display list carries.
t_morphfx The same under a colour transform.
t_edit An edit text laid out inside its own bounds.
t_editwrap The same, wrapping.
t_refuse A malformed morph and edit text, refused; the shape behind them kept.

Composites
t_xtext Masked text under a colour transform.
t_xbtn A button inside a sprite.
t_xnest Nested sprite playheads.
t_xgrad A gradient stroked, masked and dimmed at once.
t_xmorph A morph inside a mask, at three ratios.
t_xedit An edit text inside a masked sprite.
t_xbmp A bitmap under a mask and a transform.

Sound
t_snd A stream sound paced by the timeline. Four clicks, each on the frame the square flashes.
t_snd_a The same soundtrack as Flash ADPCM, which is what real content of this era uses.
t_snd_ev Event sounds only, over the page's own MIDI. Loop counts and a fade.
t_snd_big A soundtrack shipped as one DefineSound, too long for a voice, so it streams.

These four are the only pages here that cannot be checked by a machine. What is being judged is whether the click and the flash happen at the same instant, and whether the drone underneath them is continuous - both of which need a person, a television and a pair of speakers. The files come from tests/swfsnd-host/mksndswf; everything about them that a host can assert, it already does.

Bitmaps
t_xbmp A bitmap fill, under a mask and a transform. The only bitmap file with a display list.

t_bmp, t_bmp_a, t_bmp_fill and t_jpeg have no page here and cannot have one. Each defines several shapes and places none of them: they are fixtures for the host renderer, which reaches a shape by index, and each of their shapes is a separate full-stage picture, so placing them all would stack four answers on top of each other. On the console they are exercised through t_xbmp and through the decoder tests, which need no display at all.

Every file is generated by tests/swf-host/mkswf and the host suite asserts an exact pixel count for each. What the console adds is the hardware path: the same triangles, submitted rather than rasterised. Two things are known to differ and are not defects here - a mask that is not a rectangle is clipped to its bounding box, and a morphed gradient draws as the mean of its stops.

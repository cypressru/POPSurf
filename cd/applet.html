Java applets

<applet code="Chart.class">

Field initialisers run in <init> as bytecode, so the bars come from the array the source declares. 536 instructions.

<object classid="java:Modes.class">

tableswitch, a two-dimensional array, and 64-bit arithmetic. The bar along the bottom is 20! shifted and masked, painted as a colour. 7498 instructions.

<applet code="Spiro.class">

Math.sin and Math.cos over doubles, fillPolygon over int arrays. 8450 instructions.

<applet code="Ripple.class"> — animated

start() spawns a Thread; run() loops forever calling repaint() and Thread.sleep(40). The browser runs a slice of it per frame and paints between two of its instructions.

Three classes: Bounce, Sprite, Trail

The browser walks the applet's constant pool, notices the two helper classes, fetches them, and scans those in turn before anything runs.

Exceptions: throw, catch, unwind

Six paths, each a tick or a cross. Any cross means an exception did not reach its handler.

getImage and drawImage

One image at 1:1, two scaled, and one that does not exist. Images arrive after the first paint and the applet is repainted when they do.

Mouse events — click it

Java 1.0's mouseDown. Point at the box and press A; a marker drops where the cursor is and the counter goes up.

A class that is not there

A broken applet is a box that says so, never a broken page.
